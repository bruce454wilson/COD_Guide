A quick guide to finding your way around Dance Explorer.

Podcasts

Browse every guest who's appeared on Conversations on Dance from the Podcasts menu — by guest, by dancer, by choreographer, by company, and more. Themed collections (Vail Dance Festival, Live Events, Balanchine dancers, and others) group episodes a different way.

Each guest entry links out to their company profile page, Instagram, and — where available — the YouTube clip for that appearance.

Performances

The Performances menu tracks the 2026–27 season for major dance companies across the country. Four views cover the same data: By Month (chronological), By Company, By Choreographer, and By Work. The Grid gives a visual season-at-a-glance calendar.

On By Month, By Company, and the Grid, use Select companies to choose which companies you care about, then switch the All/Select toggle to Select to hide the rest. Your selection is remembered across all three pages.

Settings

The gear icon opens a Settings page for options you won't need often — currently, whether to show programs that have already ended. More options will land there as new features ship.
